About Naz Ventures

Every venture is different. The way we build them isn't.

Naz Ventures started with a single coffee counter and grew, one deliberate step at a time, into a family of hospitality and business ventures — each independent, all improving together.

Our Story

From one café to an ecosystem

Naz Ventures began with Naz Coffee — a café built on the basics done well: good coffee, fast service, a place people wanted to return to. As that business found its rhythm, the same operator instincts that made it work started showing up in new forms.

Naz Coffee Cart Service brought that same experience to weddings and events. Kaizen Spaces opened up room for workshops and community. Flow State Studio turned years of hands-on café operations into consulting for other owners. Ally Cafe Solutions started supplying the equipment and essentials that keep cafés like ours — and others' — running.

Today, that same pattern continues. The Onoy's Cloud Kitchen concept and DOSE Franchising are both in motion, built on everything the earlier ventures have already learned.

"Every business we start has to earn its place the same way the first one did — by making things better, one small improvement at a time."
— Patrick Sutare, Founder
What Guides Us

Built on Kaizen

Kaizen means continuous, incremental improvement — it's the philosophy that named one venture and quietly runs through all of them.

Continuous Improvement

Small, steady changes compound. Every venture is expected to get a little better, every day, rather than chase one big fix.

Operator-First

Every business is built by people who work the counter, staff the cart, and answer the consulting calls themselves first.

Community & Craft

Whether it's a cup of coffee or a rented workshop space, the goal is the same: create something worth showing up for.

Intentional Growth

New ventures launch when they're ready, not before — Onoy's Cloud Kitchen and DOSE Franchising are being built with the same care.
