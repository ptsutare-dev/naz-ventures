What this study was about

Naz Coffee is planning Naz Loop: a monthly coffee plan delivered to workplaces in reusable cups that we collect, wash and refill.

To learn what matters to people, participants were shown one of four versions of the page. Some versions described Naz Loop mainly as convenient. Others described it as a shared ritual that avoids single-use cups. The cup-kit terms also differed: some saw a refundable deposit, others a free kit with a fee for lost cups.

Comparing the groups shows which description and which terms people prefer. Nothing you answered commits you to buy anything.
