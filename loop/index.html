Step 1 of 2 · Informed consent

Before you begin

Study title

Testing marketing messages for a reusable-cup workplace coffee subscription in the Baybay Circuit

Researcher

Patrick Sutare, MS in Entrepreneurship student, De La Salle University (ENT602M Entrepreneurial Marketing)

Purpose

To learn how workers respond to different descriptions of a proposed coffee subscription service.

What you will do

View one web page and answer about 25 questions on your phone. This takes about 8 minutes.

Voluntary participation

Taking part is your choice. You may stop at any time without penalty. Choosing not to take part will not affect your relationship with Naz Coffee or with Balian Community College.

Risks and benefits

There are no known risks beyond everyday life. You may join a raffle for one of five free months of Loop Lite.

Information you should know

Different participants see different versions of the page. This is part of the study design and is explained at the end. Reserving a founding slot is free and does not commit you to buy.

Confidentiality

Answers are recorded without your name. Your mobile number, if you join the raffle, is kept separately and deleted after the draw. Data are handled under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), used only for this academic requirement and Naz Coffee's planning, and reported only as group results.

Questions

Contact the researcher, Patrick Sutare, through Naz Coffee's official page, or the course instructor, Dr. Louie A. Divinagracia, De La Salle University.
