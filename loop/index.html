Step 1 of 2 · Informed consent

Before you begin

Study title

Which Naz Loop offer do our regulars prefer? A study of a reusable-cup workplace coffee subscription in the Baybay Circuit

Researcher

Patrick Sutare, MS in Entrepreneurship student, De La Salle University (ENT602M Entrepreneurial Marketing)

Purpose

To learn which version of a proposed coffee subscription Naz Coffee's regular customers prefer, and how their coffee habits relate to their choice.

What you will do

Read four short versions of the offer, rate each one, then answer questions about price and your coffee habits. There are about 40 quick questions, and it takes about 10 minutes.

Voluntary participation

Taking part is your choice. You may stop at any time without penalty. Choosing not to take part will not affect your relationship with Naz Coffee or with Balian Community College.

Risks and benefits

There are no known risks beyond everyday life. Everyone who finishes receives the same small thank-you treat, whatever their answers. You may also join a raffle for one of three Loop cups personalized with your name.

Information you should know

The four versions are shown in a different order to different people. This is part of the study design. Reserving a founding slot is free and does not commit you to buy.

Confidentiality

Answers are recorded without your name. Your mobile number, if you join the raffle, is kept separately and deleted after the draw. Data are handled under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), used only for this academic requirement and Naz Coffee's planning, and reported only as group results.

Questions

Contact the researcher, Patrick Sutare, through Naz Coffee's official Facebook page.
